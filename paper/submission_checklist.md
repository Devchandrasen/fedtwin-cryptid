# IEEE Transactions on Multimedia submission checklist

## Files

- [ ] Main PDF, at most 10 pages for initial submission.
- [ ] Supplement PDF, at most four pages.
- [ ] Clean source-only Overleaf ZIP.
- [ ] Cover letter.
- [ ] Reproducibility README and reviewer artifact ZIP.
- [ ] Preprint/prior-publication disclosure.

## Content

- [ ] Abstract is 150–250 words.
- [ ] Title/abstract/contributions describe fixed-evidence calibration and audit, not a new retrieval/localization model.
- [ ] SecureAgg simulation, quantized transport proxy, and real Paillier are accurately distinguished.
- [ ] Separate VCSL visual and FMA audio tiers are not described as synchronized audio-video evidence.
- [ ] No differential-privacy, ownership, legal-infringement, production-cryptography, or state-of-the-art localization claim is made.
- [ ] Every numerical claim maps to `paper_results/v1/claims.csv` and the artifact manifest.
- [ ] Figures contain finite values and remain legible at final size.

## Metadata and policy

- [ ] Final author order, affiliations, emails, ORCIDs, and ScholarOne records agree.
- [ ] All authors approve the submission and possible mandatory page charges.
- [ ] The paper uses TMM's current single-anonymized policy wording.
- [ ] April 2026 TMM-listed EDICS are selected: `MM-COM-SECU` primary, `MM-MOD-DIST` secondary, and `MM-DAT-SEAR` tertiary.
- [ ] Code/data and prior-publication disclosures are complete.
