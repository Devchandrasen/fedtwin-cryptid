Dear Editor-in-Chief,

Please consider the manuscript "FedTwin-CryptID: Federated Fixed-Evidence Calibration and Audit for Multimedia Copy Review" for publication in IEEE Transactions on Multimedia.

The manuscript studies a bounded systems question: whether compact evidence produced by existing multimedia copy-retrieval and audio-fingerprinting pipelines can be calibrated across non-IID clients while raw media and raw embeddings remain local, and whether review decisions can be accompanied by signed media-free audit receipts. The contribution is a privacy-aware calibration and audit layer, not a new retrieval representation, copy-localization network, synchronized audio-video model, formal differential-privacy mechanism, or production secure-aggregation protocol.

The evaluation separates VCSL visual and FMA audio tiers, reports fixed-evidence baselines, calibration and rare-prevalence risk, client heterogeneity and personalization, large candidate-pool ranking, multi-round poisoning stress, SecureAgg-simulation dropout behavior, real 2048-bit Paillier compact-update aggregation, and receipt tamper detection. The artifact contains source code, permitted derived results, configurations, hashes, and table/figure regeneration metadata; restricted raw media are not redistributed.

Before this letter is used, all authors must confirm that the work is original and not under review elsewhere, approve the final submission and disclosure statements, and verify the final author metadata, ORCIDs, prior-publication details, and archival artifact DOI.
